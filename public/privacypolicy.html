Gym Log and your data

Gym Log is a workout log. You sign in to it, log your training, and its Android app can also read data from Health Connect on your phone, only after you allow it there.

Signing in

You sign in with your email address (by an emailed link or a password), or with your Google account. With Google, Google shares your email address, name and profile picture with Gym Log’s sign-in service; Gym Log uses only the email address, to know which account is yours.

What you log

Your workouts (sets, reps and weights), warm-ups, cardio, steps, body weight, waist, knee pain scores, notes and your plan, as you enter them.

If you turn on Log sets by voice, your browser turns what you say into text. In Chrome that happens on Google’s servers. In the Android app, the phone’s speech recognition does it: on the phone itself where the phone supports that, and otherwise through the phone’s speech service (usually Google’s), which may use its servers. The app asks for the microphone when you turn this on, and listens only after you tap a lift’s microphone, for one phrase. Gym Log keeps only the numbers it heard.

What it reads from Health Connect

  • Steps (by the day and by the hour), distance and floors climbed
  • Active, resting and total calories burned, and calories eaten
  • Water
  • Heart rate, resting heart rate and heart rate variability
  • Blood oxygen, breathing rate, VO₂ max and blood pressure
  • Weight, body fat and height
  • Sleep, with its stages and times
  • Exercise sessions

Each is read only if you allow it in Health Connect, and each is shown to you in the app. Gym Log never writes to Health Connect.

If you turn on Sync in the background, the app reads the last few days about once an hour while it’s closed, so the numbers stay current. It stops when you turn that off or sign out.

Where it goes

The app adds the numbers up by day and saves them, with everything you log, to your account in the database this copy of Gym Log is set up with: a Supabase project run by whoever runs this copy, hosted in the region they chose when they created it. The connection is encrypted. Only you can read your data: the database lets each account see its own rows and nobody else’s.

Nothing is shared with anyone else, sold, or used for advertising, credit, insurance or employment decisions.

Gym Log is open source, and anyone can run their own copy. This page describes what the app does; who runs the copy you are using, and where its database is, is up to them. If you run one, say so here and give people a way to reach you.

Stopping it

Turn off Sync in the background in Settings, and remove Gym Log’s access in Health Connect at any time. Data already saved stays in your Gym Log account until you delete it.
