Gym Log and your health data

Gym Log is a personal workout log. Its Android app can read data from Health Connect on your phone, only after you allow it there.

What it reads

  • Steps, distance and active calories
  • Heart rate and resting heart rate
  • Weight
  • Sleep
  • Exercise sessions

It never writes to Health Connect.

What it does with it

It adds the numbers up by day and saves them to the Gym Log database of the account you’re signed in with, so they show in the app on this phone and on the web. Only you can read them: the database lets each account see its own rows and nobody else’s.

Nothing is shared with anyone else, sold, or used for advertising.

Stopping it

Remove Gym Log’s access in Health Connect at any time. Data already saved stays in your Gym Log account until you delete it there.
